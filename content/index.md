# Pulse Width Modulation for Automotive Electronics

The complete interactive guide to PWM signals in modern vehicles. Master diagnosis, repair, and modification techniques.

## Why Learn PWM?

Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) is the primary method for controlling power delivery in modern vehicles. From radiator fans to fuel pumps, understanding PWM signals is essential for diagnosing and repairing automotive electrical systems.

### What You'll Master

- **Diagnose PWM-controlled components** with a multimeter or oscilloscope
- **Design custom motor speed controllers** with Arduino/ESP32
- **Read automotive wiring schematics** and identify PWM control lines
- **Understand switch positioning** (low-side vs high-side)
